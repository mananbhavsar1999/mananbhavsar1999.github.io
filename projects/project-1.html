Overview

The Challenge

Power plant auxiliary systems are the backbone of any steam turbine plant — they keep the main equipment running safely and efficiently. The challenge was to design, model, and document complete auxiliary piping systems including lube oil, cooling water, and steam condensate lines, ensuring every component met strict engineering codes and integration requirements.

Working within a large multi-disciplinary team at L&T Technology Services, the task demanded not only technical accuracy but precise coordination — every pipe run, valve, and instrument had to align with the P&ID, 3D model, and site constraints simultaneously.

Approach

What I Did

  • Developed complete 3D piping layouts for auxiliary systems including lube oil supply/return, cooling water, and steam & condensate lines using Siemens NX.
  • Prepared and maintained P&IDs for all auxiliary sub-systems, ensuring alignment between process requirements and physical design.
  • Performed pipe sizing calculations, pressure drop analysis, and wall thickness calculations per ASME B31.1 standard.
  • Generated GA drawings, isometric drawings, and support layouts for site installation teams.
  • Coordinated with process and instrumentation teams to ensure P&ID accuracy and resolve inter-discipline clashes.
  • Prepared equipment datasheets and MTO (Material Take Off) lists for procurement.
  • Conducted CFD analysis on pump suction systems to evaluate flow distribution and identify potential cavitation risks.

Outcome

Results & Learnings

The auxiliary system designs were successfully reviewed and approved for detailed engineering, with zero major rework cycles during the design review phase. The P&IDs passed client review with minimal comments, reflecting the care taken in aligning process intent with physical design.

This project significantly deepened my understanding of power plant system integration — particularly the interdependence between rotating equipment performance and auxiliary support system design. It also strengthened my ability to work within PLM environments using Teamcenter for document control and revision management.
