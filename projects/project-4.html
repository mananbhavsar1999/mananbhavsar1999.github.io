Overview

The Challenge

A Wärtsilä marine engine propulsion system assembly package had accumulated structural issues over time — drawing callout sequences were disorganized across ~30 drawings, BOM hierarchy levels were mismatched, and certain components no longer met current environmental compliance requirements.

The task was to rationalize the entire assembly package: restructure drawing sequences into logical categories, correct BOM hierarchy mismatches in Teamcenter, replace non-compliant materials, and validate assembly integrity through tolerance stack-up analysis — all while maintaining full revision traceability.

Approach

What I Did

  • Audited ~30 assembly drawings to map existing callout sequences — identifying randomly interleaved fasteners, sub-assemblies, and piping callouts that made manufacturing sequencing ambiguous.
  • Restructured all drawing callout sequences into logical assembly categories in Siemens NX, grouping identical assembly types together and ordering connected assemblies in installation sequence.
  • Corrected multi-level BOM hierarchy mismatches in Teamcenter — migrating misclassified components (standard hardware, washers) to their correct assembly levels and updating MTO accordingly.
  • Built and fully constrained 3D assembly models in Siemens NX, resolving broken constraints, repairing corrupted part references, and remodeling components where geometry recovery was not feasible.
  • Executed environmental compliance material substitution per Wärtsilä-approved specifications — replacing seals, O-rings, and thread-locking compounds with compliant alternatives across all affected drawings.
  • Performed tolerance stack-up analysis in Siemens NX to validate assembly fit and function following component substitutions.
  • Maintained full revision traceability across all modified drawings, BOMs, and MTO packages through Teamcenter release workflows.
Assembly models, restructured drawing packages, and stack-up analysis results from this project are available for discussion during interviews. Contact me directly for a technical walkthrough.
Outcome

Results & Learnings

The rationalized drawing package eliminated assembly sequencing ambiguity, providing manufacturing teams with a clear, category-ordered installation sequence. BOM hierarchy corrections ensured MTO accuracy and downstream procurement alignment. All environmental compliance substitutions were completed with full Teamcenter traceability.

This project reinforced the critical link between drawing quality and manufacturing efficiency — and the discipline required to manage change across a large assembly package while maintaining revision control integrity throughout.
