Overview

The Challenge

Vehicle-mounted RADAR structures must withstand significant static and dynamic loads — road vibration, operational forces, and structural stresses from the mounted equipment. Every component needs to be precisely modeled for manufacture, and the assembly must be validated against both static and dynamic load conditions before production.

The scope covered 1,050+ individual components across 25 sub-assemblies — all modeled from scratch in Siemens NX, with structural and modal analysis performed in ANSYS to validate performance and drive design iterations.

Approach

What I Did

  • Developed 1,050+ 3D component models from scratch across 25 sub-assemblies for a vehicle-mounted RADAR structure in Siemens NX, covering all structural and machined components.
  • Produced complete manufacturing drawing packages for all components and assemblies, ensuring dimensional accuracy and manufacturability per ISO standards.
  • Performed static structural analysis in ANSYS to validate component strength and assembly integrity under operational load conditions.
  • Performed modal (vibration) analysis in ANSYS to identify natural frequencies and evaluate dynamic behavior of the RADAR structure under vehicle-induced excitation.
  • Interpreted static and modal analysis results, identified design areas requiring modification, and fed findings directly back into geometry changes in NX.
  • Prepared assembly-level BOMs and fabrication drawings per ISO standards to support procurement and manufacturing readiness.
3D models, analysis results, and drawing samples from this project are available for discussion during interviews. Contact me directly for a technical walkthrough.
Outcome

Results & Learnings

All 1,050+ components were modeled and fully documented, with structural and modal analysis results driving targeted design iterations before final drawing release. The analysis-to-design feedback loop ensured that structural concerns were resolved at the modeling stage rather than discovered during physical testing.

This project built strong competency in handling large-scale NX assemblies and using simulation results to make practical design decisions — discipline applicable to any rotating equipment or structural component program.
