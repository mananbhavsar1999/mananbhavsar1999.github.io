Overview

The Challenge

Pharmaceutical and industrial utility projects demand complete system engineering from first principles — heat load calculations, equipment selection, vendor negotiation, P&ID development, and procurement documentation, all delivered as a single engineer responsible for the full scope.

Across multiple HVAC and compressor/pump package projects at Adept, every deliverable from client kickoff through procurement-ready documentation was handled independently — each client with different facility requirements, designed from scratch.

Approach

What I Did

  • Designed complete HVAC systems across multiple pharmaceutical and industrial clients — chilled water, VRF, and associated piping — covering heat load calculations, equipment sizing, layout, and full documentation from first principles.
  • Engineered compressor and pump packages for multiple clients from scratch — equipment sizing, pressure/flow analysis, piping layout, and system design per API standards.
  • Conducted vendor selection and technical bid evaluation for compressor packages across all projects — comparing Atlas Copco, Ingersoll Rand, and other OEMs against project-specific technical requirements.
  • Developed P&IDs for all utility systems independently, ensuring operability, safety, and compliance with engineering codes.
  • Prepared complete procurement packages for every project — BOM, MTO, BOQ, vendor comparison matrices, and technical specifications.
  • Led client-facing kickoff meetings, presenting system design approach, equipment selection rationale, and execution plans directly to client stakeholders.
System design documentation, P&IDs, and procurement packages from these projects are available for discussion during interviews. Contact me directly for a technical walkthrough.
Outcome

Results & Learnings

All three projects were successfully taken from scope definition through to procurement-ready documentation packages. Kickoff meetings with clients established clear project scope and equipment selection rationale, reducing downstream change requests during execution.

This role established the foundation for end-to-end package engineering — covering the full workflow from client brief through equipment selection, system design, vendor coordination, and documentation release — directly applicable to EPC utility and rotating equipment scopes.
